# Exercises-application (30 hour owlhack/hackathon)


This is a workout application for people who don't have equipment and just want to work out at home.

This app provides a calorie calculator that estimates the amount of calories a person burned.

## Step 1:
Choose what part of your body you want to work out: arms, core, or legs.

## Step 2:
Choose the difficulty level you want the workout to be.

## Step 3:
You will have a 30-minute timer to do the workout, or you can skip to the next tab.

## Step 4:
After finishing the 30-minute workout or skipping ahead, it will show you the amount of time you worked out for and the difficulty level, as well as an input for your weight.

## Step 5:
After doing all of this, it will estimate the amount of calories burned.
